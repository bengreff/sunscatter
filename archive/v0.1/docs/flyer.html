A 2D space game about building an interstellar civilization. Design rockets from 148 parts, fly them with real orbital mechanics, colonize worlds across the solar system, and push toward other stars with fusion and antimatter drives at relativistic speeds—where your ship's clock drifts against Earth's and thrust fades as you approach the speed of light. The endgame is a supply chain spanning the galaxy: colonies extracting resources, refining fuel, and building the ships that carry civilization to the next star.

1:1 Real-Scale Physics

Earth LEO at 7.8 km/s. Real planetary masses, real distances. No scaled-down shortcuts.

Galactic Scope

20 celestial bodies from Sagittarius A* to Martian moons, with a 4-component Milky Way gravity model.

Deep Vehicle Editor

148 parts across 6 propellant types. Mirror symmetry, staging, fairings, per-stage delta-v.

Patched Conic Trajectories

Maneuver nodes, transfer planner, SOI transitions, and closest approach prediction.

Relativistic Flight

Lorentz thrust limiting, gravitational time dilation, split ship/Earth clocks above 1% c.

Interstellar Propulsion

Fusion, antimatter, and nuclear pulse drives. Reactors from megawatts to terawatts.

Colony Building

Extract resources, refine fuel, manufacture parts. Build system-wide supply chains.

Full Flight Systems

Staging, fuel zones, RCS, autopilot, aerodynamic drag, heating, multi-vessel tracking.

Rust · wgpu · egui · No game engine
Custom physics loop · f64 precision · 4x MSAA
