# Playtest 2026-10-01: ten minutes in low Earth orbit

For Ben. The session tests the three defaults taken while you were away: RCS-only attitude control (D078), Ap/Pe on every closed orbit (D076), and the physical engine heat path (D077). Reverse any of them in `docs/decisions.md` if they feel wrong.

## Start

From the repo root (a fresh home directory, so your own saves and settings are untouched):

```sh
mkdir -p /tmp/ss-play/saves && cp docs/plans/playtest-2026-10-01-orbit.ron /tmp/ss-play/saves/quicksave.ron && SUNSCATTER_HOME=/tmp/ss-play cargo run -p game --release
```

When the pad scene appears, press **F9** (quickload). You are in a 235 × 298 km orbit, engine off, SAS on (Stability), **debug mode off**, 16,000 kg propellant (measured: the save loaded in the sim and coasted 100 minutes between 234.5 and 298.0 km). The save comes from the scripted demo's Orbit step (`crates/game/src/demo.rs`).

Esc opens the pause menu (debug mode toggle there); H lists every key.

## Things to try

### 1. Flip retrograde on RCS alone (1 minute)
Press **3** (Retrograde hold). Watch the craft turn, then press **1** (Stability) and turn by hand with **W A S D Q E**.
- *Judge:* does a 20 t craft turning on 8 kN·m pitch/yaw (3.7 kN·m roll) feel right? It is much slower than before (the old SAS had 40 kN·m from nothing). Propellant ticks down while the thrusters fire: up to 0.63 kg/s pitch/yaw and 1.25 kg/s roll (derived from 445 N at Isp 290 s).
- *Judge:* does the hold settle without wobbling or chattering?

### 2. The map and its apsides (1 minute)
Scroll out to the map view.
- *Judge:* Ap and Pe markers each show altitude above the ellipsoid and time to reach them (screenshot 3 shows Ap; Pe is on the far side at the start). Are they where you expect on the line?

### 3. Circularise at apoapsis and watch the heat (3 minutes)
Press **2** (Prograde), warp with **.** until "Ap in" is under a minute, **/** back to 1x, then **Z** (full throttle) near Ap and **X** to cut when Pe comes up to within about 30 km of Ap (about 18 m/s, near 1.2 s at full thrust, so tap it, or use **Shift/Ctrl** for part throttle).
- *Judge:* on a longer burn, SKIN MAX climbs toward ~1400 K (the radiation-cooled nozzle bell, limit 1640 K) while INTERIOR MAX barely moves (measured on the ascent: 1399 K bell, 293 K interior, screenshot 1). Is a hot bell and a cool cabin what you expect?
- *Judge:* after **X** the bell should fade in about a minute (measured in the sim after a 2-minute full-thrust burn: 1116 K at 10 s, 890 K at 30 s, 565 K at 136 s; by hand for a bare 1 mm niobium plate ~1020, 830 and 500 K). Until 2026-09-30 it stayed at 1400 K for 10 minutes (a coast-lattice bug, fixed in f51155c).

### 4. Deorbit and parachute (5 minutes, optional)
Press **3**, burn with **Z** until Pe is below about 50 km, **X**, warp down. Press **P** when the HUD's chute line shows q under 5.5 kPa (it reads `chute stowed, q 3.2 / 5.5 kPa`).
- *Judge:* the sequence (D079, sourced from Apollo): P fires two drogues at once (`chute DROGUE (reefed)`, 10 s reefed, then full); below 3.35 km the four mains open through two reefed stages (6 s and 10 s) and the drogues are cut away (`chute MAIN (reefed)`, then `MAIN`). The canopy mark (orange cone) grows as they fill. Fire P too fast and the drogues tear (`DROGUE TORN`): above about 10 kPa, 1.39× their design load, as Apollo's tests found.
- *The chutes are sized for a light craft* (two Apollo drogues, four Apollo mains, Cd·A 1560 m²): ~7 m/s at sea level with 1 t of propellant left. This save carries 16 t: at 20 t the reefed mains tear as they open (`MAIN TORN`, measured in the sim tests), and the craft falls at its drogue-less speed. Burn most of the propellant first, or land on the engine: the Landing panel shows BURN IN and IGNITE AT. Judge whether that is a fair challenge.
- *Known and not fixed, judge whether they matter to you:* a nose-first reentry overheats the craft (demo, in debug mode: skin 1109 K against 1100, interior 664 K against 400, measured); with the weaker RCS the craft can land on its side (demo 3, measured). All are in `docs/plans/open-issues.md`.
