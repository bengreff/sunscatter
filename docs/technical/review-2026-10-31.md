## Checked and clean

- **Determinism (rule 2):** no trig, exp or log outside `sim::math` in added production lines; no `mul_add`, no glam trig helpers, no `HashMap` or `HashSet`, no parallel reductions. The six hits are in tests (`tests/hold_modes.rs:98, 110`, `tests/maneuver.rs:210` `from_rotation_arc` for a start attitude, `tests/warp_course.rs:398`, `aero/tests.rs`). `kepler::propagate` uses `math::cbrt`, `sqrt` and a fixed iteration cap; `maneuver::aligned` uses `math::cos`. The merge's event sort is a stable sort on epochs.
- **Precision (rule 3):** no absolute coordinates subtracted in the added code. The node frame (`vessel/maneuver.rs:215`) and the chutes' descent (`live.rs:512`) go through `eph.relative`.
- **D129:** the maneuver program's wake and the scheduled programs' due epochs stop a continuous advance as an order does (`ship_tick.rs:118–130`). A live vessel acts at the first tick at or after them (`live.rs:364`). `Schedule::Every` puts each epoch at `from + k·period` with its own rounding, with no accumulation. The target and v_go compare states carried to the node's epoch. Warp-invariance §6 point 5 is closed (except in finding 7).
- **Partition and split:** every field of `ShipState` and of `Vessel` has a named rule, enforced by exhaustive destructuring. Split and merge are inverses for a split's pieces (finding 5 covers independent vessels).
- **Typed handles:** `Owner` resolves both ways in the whole and in a piece (a table test, `craft/handle.rs`). Stage indices from queued orders are bounds-checked (`program::has_run`, `edit_sequence`).
- **Patchwork:** no craft or part name is checked in changed production code; the only hits are in the small-rocket fixture (`craft/small.rs`).
- **The engine rule:** writes to engine and mechanism state are only in `command`, `propulsion` and `failure` (and the estimate's copy, finding 4).
- **Layer 2:** the launch commit decides on the controllers' readouts (`commit.rs`). The chutes read `Knowledge`'s altitude and navigation and are pure functions with a table test. Programs read through `Context::read` (the truth at `Knowledge`'s precision).
