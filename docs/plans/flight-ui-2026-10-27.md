## Contracts between BAR and NAV

- The right edge belongs to BAR: the staging bar, with cards stacked to its left. NAV's menus all live in the left dock, its notifications in a corner that is not the right edge. BAR exports the bar's width (`stage_bar::WIDTH`) for NAV's layout.
- Links into the systems screen go through one entry point NAV adds (open at a layer and/or a part); BAR's cards use it for "more".
- TWR, mass, thrust and mean Isp leave the Propulsion section for the bar (BAR); RCS leaves it for the RCS menu (NAV). NAV removes the Propulsion section once both have landed.

## Open for the owner

- Decided by NAV (logical default): the RCS menu follows RCS, so its × turns RCS off (hover says so); it has no key and is not in the menu bar. The Crew menu's limits (cabin under 90 / 70 kPa, O₂ under 18 / 16 kPa, CO₂ from 1 / 2 kPa amber / red) are rough physiology, not from a standard: say if you want NASA-STD-3001's.
- Answered 2026-10-27: plain 1–0 open menus; the hold modes and the action-group bar's functions are click-only ("Not everything needs a key").
- Decided by VESSEL (D112, logical defaults): a script's controls for a crewed vessel travel to its crew with the light delay (D067); for an uncrewed one they are set at once and its helm delays them (D034), never twice. Above physics warp every vessel's rotation input is ignored, not only the active one's. `[`/`]` start from the active vessel, nearest first, and keep that order while stepping (back and forth return the same vessels); a switch from elsewhere or a changed fleet starts a new order.
- Decided by VESSEL (D112, logical default): on a separation the piece that keeps the vessel (the one with the lowest control part: Dragon's) keeps all its controls; every other piece (the booster) keeps SAS and its hold, with the throttle closed and no rotation.
- Answered 2026-10-27: the Falcon 9 keeps its real RCS configuration (the booster's cold gas and Dragon's Dracos start disabled, its sequence enables them): "Use the real default RCS config and whatever is logical." All thrusters are on by default only where a craft file says nothing.
